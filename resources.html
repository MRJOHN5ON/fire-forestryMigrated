Educational Resources for Wildfire Mitigation and Forest Management

At Givens Fire & Forestry LLC, we believe in empowering homeowners and landowners with the knowledge they need to protect their properties. Below are trusted resources for wildfire prevention, forest management, and general safety measures.

Montana State University Extension Forestry Calendar

The Montana State University Extension Forestry Calendar is a comprehensive resource for forestry and natural resource management events, workshops, and education throughout Montana.

Learn More

NIFC Wildfire Incident Situation Report

The National Interagency Fire Center situation report offers daily insight into active fires, fire weather conditions, and resources being mobilized nationwide.

Learn More

Fire Weather & Avalanche Center

The Fire Weather & Avalanche Center provides real-time wildfire forecasts, maps, and hazard analysis to help you understand local risk.

Learn More

Firewise USA

Firewise USA offers resources and practical steps for homeowners and communities reducing wildfire risk around homes.

Learn More

ANCHOR POINT PODCAST

The Anchor Point Podcast shares discussions on fire behavior, safety protocols, and the challenges faced by wildland firefighters.

Learn More

HOTSHOT WAKE UP BLOG

The Hotshot Wake Up offers field-informed perspectives from the world of wildland firefighting.

Learn More

Wildland Fire Learning Portal

The Wildland Fire Learning Portal provides training and resources for firefighters, homeowners, and community members.

Learn More

Montana DNRC Hazardous Fuels Reduction Grants

Montana DNRC grants may help landowners reduce hazardous fuels, thin trees, remove brush, and create defensible space.
