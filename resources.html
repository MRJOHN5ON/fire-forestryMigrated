Knowledge that protects your property

At Givens Fire & Forestry LLC, we believe in empowering homeowners and landowners with the knowledge they need to protect their properties. Below are trusted resources for wildfire prevention, forest management, and general safety measures — whether you’re just starting out or deepening your mitigation plan.

Montana & Local

Montana State University Extension Forestry Calendar

The Montana State University Extension Forestry Calendar is a comprehensive resource for individuals interested in forestry and natural resource management. It provides a detailed schedule of upcoming events, workshops, and educational opportunities throughout Montana, catering to both landowners and professionals.

Participants can engage in activities designed to enhance their understanding of forest health, fire prevention, and sustainable land management — covering forest ecology, fire and insect management, wildlife habitat, understory management, and Forest Stewardship planning.

Fire Intelligence

NIFC Wildfire Incident Situation Report

The National Interagency Fire Center (NIFC) Incident Management Situation Report (IMSR) is a crucial daily publication with in-depth insights into wildfire incidents across the United States — active fires, fire weather conditions, and resources being mobilized. Staying current helps homeowners, land managers, and responders anticipate threats and understand coordinated protection efforts.

Fire Intelligence

Map of Fire

Map of Fire is a live wildfire map for tracking active fires, smoke, and lightning — with layers, incident reports, and dashboards to help you monitor wildfire activity and risk in your area.

Home & Community

Firewise USA

Firewise USA, developed by the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA), empowers communities to reduce wildfire risks with practical strategies for defensible space, hazard reduction, and neighborhood resilience. An essential starting point for homeowners protecting property from wildfire.

Field Voices

Anchor Point Podcast

The Anchor Point Podcast offers deep discussions for the wildland firefighting community — fire behavior, safety protocols, mental health, and candid interviews with industry professionals. A platform for knowledge, reflection, and connection within the fire community.

Field Voices

Hotshot Wake Up Blog

Hotshot Wake Up delivers unfiltered perspectives from wildland firefighting — the challenges, lessons, and human side of the job. The show emphasizes community support, resilience, and education around mental and physical well-being in high-stakes work.

Training

Wildland Fire Learning Portal

This portal serves as a comprehensive hub for wildland firefighters and the public — courses on fire prevention, safety protocols, and best practices for reducing wildfire risk. Valuable tools for firefighters advancing their skills and homeowners building preparedness.

Montana & Local

Montana DNRC Hazardous Fuels Reduction Grants

The Montana Department of Natural Resources and Conservation offers grants to help landowners reduce hazardous fuels — brush removal, tree thinning, and defensible space work. You may qualify for funding toward the mitigation services our team provides.
