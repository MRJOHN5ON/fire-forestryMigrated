Our Services: Protecting Montana, One Property at a Time

  • Defensible Space Consulting

    From former wildland firefighters, we deliver no-nonsense defensible space solutions. We assess, design, and clear hazardous areas, creating a fire-safe perimeter that keeps your property protected from wildfires. Trust our expertise to make your home fire-resistant and secure.

    Learn more!

  • Slash Pile Removal

    We specialize in efficient slash pile removal, reducing fire hazards and improving forest health. Our expert team removes debris, clears excess vegetation, and ensures your property is safe from wildfire threats. Let us handle the heavy lifting while you gain peace of mind.

    Learn more!

  • Hazardous Tree Removal

    Hazardous trees, including dead, fire-damaged, leaning, diseased, or insect-infested trees, are a major wildfire risk. Our expert team safely removes these high-risk trees using advanced equipment to reduce fire hazards and protect your property.

    Learn more!

  • Post burn Clean-Up

    After a wildfire, cleanup is crucial to prevent further risks like erosion, pest infestations, and lingering fire hazards. Our expert team removes charred debris, dead vegetation, and hazardous materials to help your property recover.

    Learn more!

  • Brush Removal Services

    Overgrown brush is a major fire hazard, providing fuel for wildfires and increasing risk to your property. Our brush removal services create defensible space, reduce fire intensity, and improve land health while preserving natural beauty.

    Learn More!

  • Storm Removal

    When severe weather strikes, downed trees and debris create immediate hazards and potential fire risks. Our storm removal service quickly clears fallen timber, damaged branches, and storm-related debris from your property.
