Nominate a Neighbor Program – Giving Back to Those Who Give to Others

Protect & Honor Our Community!

Givens Fire and Forestry is pleased to offer a special opportunity to support our community. Through our Nominate a Neighbor Program, we are providing two complimentary days of fire mitigation work to a deserving neighbor—someone who has made a meaningful impact on those around them.

This initiative is our way of recognizing someone who has made a meaningful impact—whether through kindness, service, or dedication to others. Do you know a neighbor who could use help protecting their property from wildfire risks? Now’s your chance to recognize them!

Nominations are open until June 30th, and after reviewing submissions, we’ll schedule a consultation with the selected recipient to determine the best plan for their property.

Join us in making our community safer while honoring those who go above and beyond for others!
