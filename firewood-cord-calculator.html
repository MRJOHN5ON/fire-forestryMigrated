What is a Cord?

A cord is the standard firewood unit in the U.S. and Canada: 4′ high × 4′ wide × 8′ long (128 cubic feet, or 221,184 cubic inches).

A full cord is typically 600–800 split pieces and roughly 2 tons, depending on species and moisture content.

This tool uses a frustum formula for tapered logs — ideal for whole-tree estimates after felling.
